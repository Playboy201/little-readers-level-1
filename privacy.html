Privacy Policy

Effective 28 July 2026

This page explains how information may be handled when you visit the Little Readers Level 1 website or purchase the digital product.

Information collected

The website may receive technical and marketing information such as your device type, browser, approximate location, referral source and UTM campaign parameters. Information submitted during checkout is handled by the secure checkout provider.

How information is used

  • To provide access to your purchase and customer support.
  • To understand website performance and advertising results.
  • To prevent fraud, protect the service and meet applicable obligations.

Service providers

The website uses third-party services for checkout, campaign attribution and video delivery, including EscalePay, UTMify and ConverteAI. These providers may process data according to their own privacy terms. PB Teaching does not collect or store payment card details on this website.

Cookies and tracking

Campaign attribution tools may use cookies or similar browser storage. You can restrict these technologies through your browser settings, although some attribution features may no longer work.

Your choices

You may request information about personal data connected with your order, or ask for a correction or deletion where applicable. Use the support details supplied in your order confirmation so the request can be matched securely to your purchase.

Updates

This policy may be updated when the website, providers or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

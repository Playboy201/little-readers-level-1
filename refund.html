Digital Product Refund Policy

Effective 28 July 2026

Little Readers Level 1 is a digital product delivered shortly after confirmed payment. No physical item is shipped.

After digital access is supplied

Because a digital file cannot be returned once access has been supplied, purchases are final after delivery or download, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

Access problems

If you do not receive access or the supplied file cannot be opened, contact support using the details in your order confirmation. PB Teaching will first verify the order and make reasonable efforts to restore access or provide a working file.

Duplicate or incorrect charges

Verified duplicate charges or payment errors will be reviewed with the checkout provider. Include the order reference and payment information requested by support, but never send full payment card details.

Requesting assistance

Use the support channel included in your payment or order confirmation. Requests should identify the purchaser, order reference and the problem experienced. Nothing in this policy limits rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.
